# UX brief

Read `.claude/pm-conventions.md` first and follow its names, paths and formats exactly. Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`.

1. Read the PRD and research.
2. Write: user goals, key scenarios (3–5, step by step), entry points, states to design (empty, loading,
   error, permission denied), content needs, accessibility requirements, open design questions, and what
   success looks like for the design.
3. Write `ux-briefs/<stem>_<date>.md`.
Finish by writing the checkpoint `<slug>/checkpoints/ux-brief.md` and telling the PM the next step.
